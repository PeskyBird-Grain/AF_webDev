Goblin Valley

Goblin Valley is a State park located in Emery County, Utah. It is characterized by it's many hoodoos, which form when soft sandstone is eroded to create a rocky pillar. These create an otherworldly landscape. It is also a great place for hiking, camping, and overall exploration.

Reasons to go:

  • You are free to explore, and aren't stuck on one specific path.
  • The landscape is fascinating and unique.
  • It's a designated International Dark Sky Park.
  • It's fun!

Goblin Valley at Night

Goblin Valley is a designated International Dark Sky Park. This means that there's so little light pollution that you can see the Milky Way at night! It's an amazing spot for stargazing and astrophotography.
